# FinTrack

## About 

FinTrack is a personal finance management application designed to help users understand where their money goes and make more informed financial decisions. Users can track their income and expenses, monitor their spending throughout the month, and understand how their financial behavior affects their personal goals.

Beyond expense tracking, FinTrack aims to provide goal-oriented financial insights, helping users adjust their spending according to objectives such as building savings, planning a trip, buying a car, or starting to invest.

## Goals 

FinTrack aims to help users:

- Understand where their money is going.
- Maintain visibility over their income and expenses throughout the month.
- Define financial goals and track their progress.
- Identify spending habits that may be preventing them from reaching their goals.
- Receive actionable insights based on their financial situation and personal objectives.

## Initial Features

- Record income
- Edit income
- Record expenses
- Edit expenses
- Delete expenses
- View monthly financial summary
- Export financial summary as PDF
- Create financial goals
- Track financial goal progress
